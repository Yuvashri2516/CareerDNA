STAR Method Breakdown

1. Situation (15%)

Set the context, team size, role, and problem background concisely.

2. Task (15%)

Describe your explicit responsibility and what goal was required.

3. Action (50%)

Detail the specific steps, technologies, tools, and decisions YOU made.

4. Result (20%)

Share quantifiable outcomes (e.g., +40% speed, $10k saved, team approval).
